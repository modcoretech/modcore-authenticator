# modcore Authenticator

A secure, modern two-factor authentication (2FA) extension for your browser. 

modcore Authenticator keeps your accounts safe by storing your 2FA codes locally on your device, encrypted with a passkey that only you can unlock. No cloud servers, no tracking, and no external services ever see your secret keys.

## Features

- **Passkey Security:** Unlock your vault using your device's built-in biometrics (Face ID, Touch ID, Windows Hello).
- **Seamless Autofill:** Automatically detects 2FA input fields on websites and offers a quick menu to insert your current code.
- **Encrypted Vault:** Your accounts are heavily encrypted and stored only on your device. 
- **Side Panel Support:** Keep your codes open and visible in the browser's side panel while you work.
- **Backup and Restore:** Safely export your accounts to an encrypted backup file so you never lose access.
- **Modern Interface:** A clean, dark-mode-first design that is easy on the eyes and simple to navigate.

## Getting Started

1. Install the extension.
2. Open modcore Authenticator and click "Create Passkey" to secure your vault.
3. **Important:** Save the recovery code that is generated. This is the only way to recover your account if you lose your device.
4. Start adding your accounts by scanning a QR code, uploading an image, or entering the setup key manually.

## Privacy First

Your secrets never leave your device. modcore Authenticator does not communicate with any external servers. When you add an account, the data is encrypted immediately and stored locally in your browser. The extension only decodes your codes locally and never transmits them anywhere.

## Recovery

Because we do not store your data on a server, we cannot reset your passkey for you. If you lose your passkey, you must use the recovery code you saved during setup to regain access. Keep it somewhere safe and offline.
